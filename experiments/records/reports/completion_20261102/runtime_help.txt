usage: case_runtime.py [-h] {prepare,nodes,coordinate} ...

One Bank case: causal replay, all node workers, coordinator, and final answer.
CPU replay runs in a separate process and publishes immutable prefix frames.
Local 4B and 9B phases share one GPU sequentially. This is accelerated replay
with queued inference, not a claim about real-time latency or physical
devices. No labels or scorer are imported here; case_eval is a separate final
process.

positional arguments:
  {prepare,nodes,coordinate}

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
